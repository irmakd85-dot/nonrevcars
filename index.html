Frequently asked questions

How much can I actually save?+

Members report saving 20–40% versus standard public rates, depending on the destination, provider and dates. Because the rates and availability change constantly, the search above always shows you the live price for your trip.

Are there any booking or card fees?+

No. There are no hidden fees, no credit card fees, and no amendment fees. You see the price you pay.

Who can use these airline staff car rental discounts?+

The crew rates are aimed at airline employees — cabin crew, pilots, ground and operations staff, and in many cases their immediate family. Anyone can book through the site.

Which car rental companies can I book?+

Bookings are fulfilled through leading global providers including Hertz, Avis, Europcar, Alamo, National and Thrifty, compared side by side so you get the best available rate, across more than 6,000 locations worldwide.

Do I pay the full amount upfront?+

No — you secure the booking with a small advance payment and pay the balance when you collect the car.

Can I change or cancel if my roster changes?+

Yes. Amendments are free, and free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before your rental begins.
