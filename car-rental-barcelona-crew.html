Car hire in Barcelona for crew on a break

Last reviewed August 2026 by the NonRevCars editorial team · about a 4-minute read

Barcelona's metro and walkable old town mean you don't need a car for the city itself — but a car is exactly what unlocks the rest of Catalonia on a day or two off: the Costa Brava coastline, the Penedès wine country, or a fast run up to the Pyrenees foothills. If you've got more than a city stroll's worth of time, this is where hiring a car pays off.

Why bother with a car here

Skip the car if you're only doing the Gothic Quarter, Sagrada Família and the beach — that's all walkable or a short metro ride. But if your layover stretches to a full day off, Barcelona is a brilliant base for day trips: Sitges' beaches are 40 minutes south, Montserrat's mountain monastery is under an hour, and the Costa Brava's coves start opening up a little further north. None of that is realistic without your own wheels.

What to know about driving here

  • Driving side: right-hand side, same as most of continental Europe.
  • City parking is tight and metered in most central zones — if you're only exploring the city itself, a car is more hassle than help. Save it for the day trips.
  • Tolls apply on several Catalan motorways (autopistas) — worth having a card ready or checking if your rental includes a tag.
  • El Prat Airport (BCN) has rental desks in both terminals, generally efficient.

Good use of a day off

A popular loop: head to Sitges for a beach morning, then loop inland through the Penedès wine region for lunch, back into the city by evening. Or go the other direction — Girona's old town and the Costa Brava coves make for a full, unhurried day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a car to get around Barcelona itself?+

No — the metro and walking cover the city well. A car is most useful for day trips outside the city, like the Costa Brava or Montserrat.

Is parking difficult in central Barcelona?+

Yes, it's tight and metered in most central areas. If you're staying in the city, consider leaving the car for day-trip days rather than driving in daily.

Are there tolls on the roads near Barcelona?+

Yes, several Catalan motorways charge tolls. Have a card ready or check whether your rental includes an electronic toll tag.
