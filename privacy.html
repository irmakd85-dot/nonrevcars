Privacy and cookie notice

Publication draft: Business operator identity, privacy contact details, relevant retention periods and third-party provider terms require confirmation before publishing this page.

NonRevCars (Airline Staff Car Rentals) is an independent car rental referral website. We provide car hire information and link visitors to an external booking partner. This notice explains the principal third-party services used by the site.

What the site uses

  • Google Analytics 4: if you accept analytics, we measure page visits and direct links to the booking search website to improve our content. Google may process technical and usage data and use cookies. You can reject this optional analytics tracking.
  • CarRentalNet: our site embeds search and promotional widgets and links to the booking platform at airlinecarrentals.carrentalnet.com. Interacting with these third-party services may involve collection of information under the booking provider's own privacy terms. We do not receive your full booking details merely because you visit our website.
  • MailerLite: newsletter sign-up is optional. The MailerLite script is requested when you choose to open the newsletter signup form. If you subscribe, the contact details you submit are processed by MailerLite to manage your subscription. MailerLite may process additional technical data when its signup form loads; its cookies and tracking behaviour require verification before this draft notice is published.
  • Website hosting: our site is deployed using Vercel, which may process technical request and security information.

Analytics preferences

We don't load the Google Analytics tag unless you've actively chosen to accept analytics. You can use the Privacy settings button displayed on our pages to change your decision. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from reading the site.

External services and links

External booking links and embedded content can operate under third-party privacy and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing the partner's current terms before entering any personal or payment details.

How to contact us

For privacy questions, email info@airlinecarrentals.com. Registered business name: Airline Staff Car Rentals.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if our use of analytics or third-party services changes. We will verify its contents and publication date before launch.
