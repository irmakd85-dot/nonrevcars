Does Thrifty have an airline staff discount?

Last reviewed August 2026 by the NonRevCars editorial team · about a 4-minute read

Thrifty positions itself as the budget-conscious option among the major rental brands — a solid pick when you just need reliable wheels for a layover without paying for extras you don't need.

Does Thrifty have an airline staff discount?

There's no single universal crew-wide rate advertised across the board. Thrifty is part of the same corporate family as Hertz, and its value proposition is generally a lower base price rather than a dedicated staff programme. The reliable way to know the real price is to compare it live against other providers for your dates.

Who this covers

  • Cabin crew and flight attendants
  • Pilots and flight deck
  • Ground, ops and airport staff
  • Air traffic control
  • Retired airline and airport staff
  • Immediate family, in most cases

Carrying proof of your travel-industry connection — an airline or airport ID, a company letter, or similar — is worth doing at pickup regardless of provider.

Why crew consider Thrifty

Thrifty tends to run a leaner fleet and simpler counter experience than some bigger-name brands, which usually translates to a lower base rate — a sensible choice on a routine layover where you just need a basic, reliable car rather than anything fancy.
