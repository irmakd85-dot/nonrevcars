How to actually choose

  • Tight layover? National's Emerald Aisle or any provider with a fast on-airport counter.
  • Flying somewhere unfamiliar? Hertz or Avis for their sheer network size.
  • Based in or flying around Europe? Europcar's regional depth is hard to beat.
  • Want the brand most associated with crew rates specifically? Alamo's reputation is the longest-standing.
  • Price is the priority? Thrifty tends to run the leanest base rate.

Whichever you lean toward, the honest advice is the same: compare the live price for your specific dates rather than assuming — availability and pricing shift constantly, and the "best" provider often changes trip to trip.
