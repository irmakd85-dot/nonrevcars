Do airline employees get car rental discounts?

Last updated June 2026 · about a 5-minute read

Short answer: yes — but not in the way they used to. If you've worked in the industry for a while, you might remember when nearly every major rental company had an obvious "airline staff rate" you could pull up at the counter. Those blanket discounts have mostly quietly disappeared. The good news is that the savings haven't vanished — they've just moved. Today the real crew rates live with specialist booking partners rather than directly on the big rental brands' own counters.

Here's what's actually true in 2026, and how cabin crew, pilots and ground staff are still saving real money on car hire.

What changed (and why it confuses people)

For years, the big rental brands ran their own airline-staff promotions. Most have since pulled or buried them, which is why so many crew members search this exact question and come away unsure. A few brand-specific offers still exist in certain regions, but they're patchy, region-locked, and often smaller than people expect.

What's replaced them is simpler than it sounds: specialist booking platforms negotiate crew-friendly rates across many providers at once, then make those rates available to airline staff in one place. Instead of hunting brand by brand, you compare them together and book the best one.

How much can airline staff actually save?

Realistically, members report saving in the region of 20–40% versus standard public rates, though the exact figure depends on three things:

  • Where you're renting — savings tend to be larger in North America than in parts of Europe.
  • Which provider and car class — the gap varies between suppliers and vehicle types.
  • How far ahead you book — prices climb closer to the pick-up date, so booking early genuinely helps.

Because rates move constantly, the honest answer to "what will I save?" is always: check the live price for your specific trip. A figure on a marketing page is just an average; the live search is the truth.

Who actually qualifies?

This is the question that trips people up, because the term "airline staff" is broader than most assume. Crew rates are generally aimed at the wider travel-industry community, which typically includes:

  • Cabin crew and flight attendants
  • Pilots and flight deck
  • Ground, operations and airport staff
  • Travel-industry professionals more broadly
  • In many cases, immediate family — and often retirees from the industry too

If you're picturing the classic "non-rev" community — the people who fly standby and live by the roster — that's exactly who these rates are built around.

Do I need a promo code or membership?

No. This is one of the most common worries, and it's a pleasant surprise: there's no code to hunt for and no paid membership wall. The crew rates are simply built into the booking. You search, you compare, you book — the rate is already there.

What about ID and proof?

For most crew bookings the rate is applied at the time of booking, but it's always sensible to carry your airline or airport ID when you collect the car, since some suppliers may ask for proof of travel-industry employment at the counter. If you don't have a formal ID card to hand, things like a company letter, business card or payslip can serve the same purpose. When in doubt, bring more than one.

A quick, practical tip from how crew actually travel: rosters change. Book a rate that lets you amend or cancel for free, so a swapped trip doesn't cost you. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pick-up is standard on crew bookings.

The simplest way to do it in 2026

Rather than checking each rental brand's site separately and hoping a staff rate still exists, the efficient route is to use a crew-focused comparison that pulls the negotiated rates together:

  1. Enter your pick-up location and dates.
  2. Compare the crew rates across providers side by side.
  3. Book the one that fits — usually with a small deposit, balance paid at pick-up.

That's genuinely the whole process. No membership, no code, no counter haggling.

Frequently asked questions

Do all airlines offer car rental discounts to staff?+

The discounts generally aren't run by individual airlines anymore — they come through specialist travel-industry booking partners and apply to airline staff broadly, regardless of which airline you work for.

Can my family use airline staff car rental rates?+

In many cases yes — immediate family can typically use the crew rates, and in North America a spouse or partner can often drive at no extra cost. Check the booking terms for your specific rental.

Do retired airline staff still qualify?+

Often, yes. Many travel-industry rates remain available to retirees from the sector, not just current employees.

Is there a fee to access these discounts?+

No. There's no membership fee and no promo code required. The crew rates are built into the booking, with no hidden or credit-card fees.
