Car rental discounts for pilots

Last updated June 2026 · about a 4-minute read

If you're a pilot wondering whether the car-hire perks apply to you specifically — they do. Pilots sit squarely within the travel-industry community these crew rates are built for. Here's how it works for flight deck, and how to make the most of it across a flying schedule.

Yes, pilots qualify

The negotiated crew rates available through travel-industry booking partners apply to airline staff broadly — pilots and flight deck very much included, alongside cabin crew and ground staff. There's no separate, lesser tier for any one role; it's the same access to rates that members report running 20–40% below standard public prices.

Why it suits flight-deck life

Pilots tend to have a few things working in their favour here. You usually know your roster well in advance, which is perfect for booking early and locking in the best rate. And the same flexibility that protects cabin crew — free amendments and cancellation up to 48 hours before pick-up — covers the reality that flight-deck schedules shift too. Book early, stay flexible, lose nothing if it changes.

Family and partners

As with the wider crew community, the rates generally extend to immediate family, and in many regions a partner can be added as a driver at no extra cost. Handy when the trip is personal rather than a layover. If a family member is collecting the car without you, it's worth checking that booking's terms and carrying suitable proof of the travel-industry connection.

For pilots the play is simple: you've got advance notice of your schedule, so book early on a flexible crew rate. You capture the best price with none of the risk.

A few flight-deck-friendly habits

  • Carry your airline ID at pick-up — some suppliers may ask for proof of travel-industry employment.
  • Pre-book airport pick-ups on tighter layovers so you're mobile the moment you land.
  • Right-size the car — smaller is cheaper to rent and fuel, and easier to park, unless the trip genuinely needs more.
